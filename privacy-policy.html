NAAD — Privacy Policy

Effective: 9 September 2026 · App: NAAD (com.heaventechnosystem.naad) · Developer: Heaven Techno System, Bhopal, India

NAAD is a devotional music and katha player. It is built to work entirely on your device. This page explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

1. Data stored on your phone only

  • Your music library, liked songs, playlists, play counts, listening minutes, downloads, resume positions and settings are stored in a local database on your phone. They never leave the device unless you sign in (see §3).
  • If you use On this device, NAAD reads the audio files on your phone (with your permission) and lists them. Files are not uploaded anywhere.

2. Permissions and why

  • Music / audio files (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO) — only to list and play your own songs when you tap "Scan phone music".
  • Notifications — only for the optional morning / evening aarti reminders you switch on, and for the playback controls notification while music plays.
  • Foreground service / wake lock — to keep music playing with the screen off.
  • Vibrate — the haptic tick in Mantra Jaap.

NAAD does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

3. Optional account & sync

You can optionally create an account (email + password) to sync your liked songs and playlists across phones. This uses Supabase. We store your email address and a JSON copy of your liked songs and playlists, protected so that only your account can read it. Nothing else is collected. You can delete your account and all synced data at any time by emailing us (below).

4. Streaming sources

Audio is streamed from third-party services when you play it: the Internet Archive (devotional recordings and katha), Audius and Jamendo (free / Creative-Commons music) and Radio Browser (live radio stations). As with any web request, those services can see your IP address. NAAD sends them no personal information.

5. No ads, no tracking, no selling of data

NAAD contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs and no trackers. We do not sell or share personal data with anyone.

6. App updates

The app may download JavaScript updates from Expo's update service (u.expo.dev) to deliver improvements without a store update. This transfers no personal data beyond the standard request metadata.

7. Children

NAAD is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
